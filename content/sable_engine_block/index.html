Sable Engine

Block ID: neoperipheral:sable_engine
Peripheral Type: neo_sable_engine (API Reference)

The Sable Engine Block connects ComputerCraft directly to the Sable physical simulation engine. When placed on a moving sub-level structure (such as an airship, vehicle, or floating platform), it acts as an onboard flight computer and thruster controller.

Capabilities

  • Telemetry & Inertial Navigation: Read exact world position, orientation quaternions, linear velocities, angular velocities, mass data, and center of mass.
  • Impulse & Thruster Control: Apply precise linear impulses, angular momentum, or localized force vectors at specific points on the sub-level to steer and accelerate the construction.
